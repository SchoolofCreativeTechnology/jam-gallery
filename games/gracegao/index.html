Tidal Island

Move with WASD or the arrow keys.

Gather sea treasures on the wet sand; fruit grows under the palms.

Waves knock you back and may make you drop an item.

When the low-tide bar fills, the sea takes the beach back.

Stand at the shop counter and press E to craft ice cream and jewelry.

The boat at the pier buys them, and your money carries to the next day.
